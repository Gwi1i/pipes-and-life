## 1. Devlog de itch (pegar en itch.io → Devlogs)

**Título**: The map came alive — flowing water, parties, and a garbage truck

> Two weeks of updates, most of them born from your feedback:
>
> - **You can see the water flow now.** Every connected pipe shows drops
>   moving in the real direction of flow — supply toward the town, sewage
>   toward the treatment plant. A dry pipe means a disconnected pipe, so
>   the map itself tells you what's wrong.
> - **The garbage truck.** Roads felt dead next to the pipes, so now a
>   little truck drives your waste routes, town to landfill.
> - **Parties.** Incorporating a village throws confetti. Money you earn
>   floats up from where you earned it.
> - **The bottleneck shows itself.** When a pipe section is choking your
>   network, it gets marked in red on the map — no more guessing which
>   segment to renew.
> - **Springs.** Mountain springs now surface as you explore: the one
>   groundwater clue that never lies. Reading the terrain can replace
>   paying for a survey.
> - **Renewing is a decision now.** Upgrading a pipeline or road includes
>   a fixed works cost (mobilizing the crew costs the same for 5 tiles as
>   for 50 — that's how it really works). You could max everything out in
>   20 minutes; now it's an investment you weigh.
> - **The Trade Book.** A collection panel of every facility you've
>   discovered, with the real-world explanation of each one.
> - Plus ~20 fixes found by playtesting (thanks to everyone who reported
>   confusing moments — the "what now?" card and several tooltips exist
>   because of you).
>
> Free, no ads, in your browser: https://gwiii.itch.io/pipes-and-life
> (Disclosure: built with AI assistance; I'm a waterworks professional
> and the water logic is the real thing.)
